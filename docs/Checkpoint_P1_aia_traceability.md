# MR ↔ AI Act traceability – Step 1 basis (8 Oct 2026)
 
Deliverable: `MR_AIA_Traceability_Step1.xlsx` (sheets: MR-AIA Matrix, AIA Requirements, Summary, Legend & Sources).
 
## Regulatory basis used
- **Digital Omnibus on AI, Reg. (EU) 2026/1744 (OJ L 24.7.2026)** moves the Machinery Regulation from AIA Annex I Section A to **Section B (point 21)**. For machinery AI, only AIA Art 6(1), 60a and 102–112 apply directly (amended Art 2(2)).
- New **MR Art 8, 3rd para**: Commission delegated acts amend MR Annex III to reflect AIA Chapter III Section 2 + Arts 17, 19, 72, 73; apply **by 2 Aug 2028**.
- New **MR Art 20(10)**: interim presumption of conformity via AIA harmonised standards / common specs (Arts 40/41) → relevant for Step 3.
- New **AIA Art 6(1a)/(1b)**: non-safety uses (performance optimisation, quality control, etc.) are not safety components unless failure endangers H&S → relevant for Step 2 (e.g. predictive maintenance).
- AIA text consolidated with Omnibus changes to Art 10(1), 10(5) (→ Art 4a), 10(6), 11(1), 17(2), 72(3), 3(14).
## Method
- 621 MR rows (all 180 Annex III sections, all 266 lettered/roman points, paragraph level elsewhere) + 89 AIA-only rows inserted after nearest EHSR.
- Overlap classes: Overlap – MR covers / Partial – AIA adds / AIA only / MR only / Definition alignment / Scope.
- AI relevance: High / Conditional (only if the function is realised by AI) / Low / None.
## Key alignment issues flagged
- MR absolute wording vs AIA probabilistic thresholds (1.2.1 ¶2(c), Art 9(8)).
- Log retention/purpose: MR 5 y / 1 y for authorities vs AIA ≥ 6 months incl. PMM (Art 19).
- MR "intended evolution" vs AIA pre-determined changes / substantial modification.
- Fundamental-rights risks: Omnibus empowerment covers "health and safety requirements" only.
- Supervisor actions limited by MR 3.2.4 vs AIA 14(4)(d) override.
